# Pitch

**Postilla: learn Italian by reading real stories.**

Postilla turns short stories into Italian lessons. Every story is written for your level, from your first words at A1 to fluent reading at C2. Each Italian sentence has its Portuguese translation right beside it, so you always know what you're reading.

Tap a word you don't know for its meaning, conjugation, pronunciation and related words, without leaving the page. Press play to hear the story read by a native voice, with each sentence highlighted as it's spoken.

Before you open a story, you see how long it takes to read, how long the audio runs, and how much of it uses everyday vocabulary, so you can pick one that's challenging but not overwhelming. Stories come in genres and topics: daily life, family, travel, mystery, and classics like Pinocchio, always credited to their original authors.

Everything works offline. New stories and corrections arrive on their own, so there's always something new to read.

**Read more. Understand more. Speak Italian.**

# Features

1. **Learn Italian through short stories.** You pick the language you're learning (Italian) and the language you read in (Brazilian Portuguese).
2. **Stories for every level.** From beginner (A1) to advanced (C2), sorted into genre shelves and tagged by topic, place and form.
3. **Translation sentence by sentence.** Each Italian sentence is paired with its translation.
4. **Tap any word.** A built-in Italian dictionary shows meanings, Portuguese translations, conjugations, pronunciation, synonyms and related words.
5. **Listen while you read.** Each story is narrated, and the sentence being read is highlighted.
6. **Know a story before you open it.** Each card shows the level, reading time, audio length, and how much of the story uses common vocabulary.
7. **Works offline.** Stories and the dictionary are kept on the phone.
8. **Updates on its own.** New and corrected stories and dictionary updates arrive by themselves, and removed stories disappear.
9. **Credits.** The original author and license are shown for adapted and classic works, such as Pinocchio.
